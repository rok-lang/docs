# E0011: nesting too deep

An expression nests deeper, or a method chain runs longer, than the compiler handles. Split it: bind a part to a `let` and continue from there.

A program has to nest hundreds of levels deep, or chain thousands of calls, to reach the limit, so this page has no example. It usually comes from generated code; have the generator emit `let` bindings for the inner parts.
