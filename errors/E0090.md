# E0090: invalid coroutine use

A coroutine (`async` or `yield`) breaks a rule: `await` on something that is not a future, or a pointer into the coroutine's frame used after a suspension point. Await a future, and take pointers again after `await` or `yield`.

The usual cause is a pointer taken with `addr_of` inside an `async` or `yield` body and used after an `await` or `yield`: the frame may have moved, so take the pointer again after the suspension point. Awaiting a value that is not a future usually reports [E0069](E0069.html), since `await` needs `IntoFuture`.
